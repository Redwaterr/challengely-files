Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This policy explains what data the Challengly mobile app ("Challengly", "the app", "we", "us") collects, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to the Challengly apps for Android and iOS and to our servers.

1. Summary

  • You sign in with Google or Apple. We do not ask for or store a password.
  • We store what you need to play: your profile, rooms, scores, chat messages and settings.
  • Your display name, room nicknames, profile photo, scores and statistics are visible to other players.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • Payments are handled by Apple and Google. We never see your card details.
  • Ads are shown by Google AdMob. Premium members see no ads.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app (Settings → Delete account).

2. Data we collect

Account data

  • Sign-in identifiers: the account identifier Google or Apple gives us when you sign in.
  • Email address: as provided by Google or Apple. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we only receive a private relay address.
  • Name and profile photo: your Google name and photo, or the name you give when signing in with Apple. You can change your display name in the app.
  • Language of the app, used for notification texts.
  • Sign in with Apple token: stored only so we can revoke Apple's sign-in access when you delete your account, as Apple requires.

Game and room data

  • Rooms you create or join, your role (owner, admin, member), the nickname you use in each room, join requests, invitations, and removals from rooms.
  • Each game you play: the game, your score, how long it took, when it started and finished, the app version, and whether the run was accepted, abandoned or rejected by our fair-play checks.
  • Your daily attempts, rankings, points, records, wins and other statistics.
  • Game ratings (1–5 stars) and game suggestions you send us, including an optional contact email.
  • Whether you have Premium, and until when.

Chat and safety data

  • Messages you send in rooms (text and GIF links), replies, emoji reactions and which messages you have read.
  • Reports you submit about messages, users or rooms, and users you block.

Device and usage data

  • Push notification token from Firebase, your device platform (Android or iOS) and app version.
  • When you last used the app, and the dates on which you used it (for your profile statistics).
  • IP address: processed by our servers and by Cloudflare to deliver the service, prevent abuse (rate limiting) and keep it secure. We do not store IP addresses in our database.

Data we do not collect

  • We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos or microphone.
  • We do not receive payment card or bank details.
  • You cannot upload images; GIFs are links to the GIF provider.

5. What other players can see

  • Your display name, profile photo, profile statistics, records and global leaderboard positions are visible to all signed-in players. Other players can find you by name to invite you to a room.
  • Inside a room, members see your room nickname, role, scores, points and the messages you send.
  • Your email address is never shown to other players.

6. Where your data is stored

Our servers and database are located in Germany (EU). Some providers listed above, such as Google, Apple, Cloudflare and RevenueCat, may process data in other countries, including the United States. Where this happens, they use legal safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

7. How long we keep data

  • While your account exists: we keep your data so the app works.
  • When you delete your account: your profile is anonymized (shown as "Deleted user"), you are removed from all rooms, and your sign-in links, tokens, devices, pending invitations and global statistics are deleted. Your Sign in with Apple access is revoked. Messages you sent and past daily results stay in their rooms, attributed to "Deleted user", so other players' history stays consistent.
  • Reports are kept as long as needed to handle them and protect our users.
  • Purchase records may be kept longer where accounting or tax law requires it.
  • Backups are overwritten within [30] days.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Delete your account: in the app under Settings → Delete account, or see how to delete your account.
  • Change your data: edit your display name, language and room nicknames in the app.
  • Notifications: mute any room in the app, or turn notifications off in your device settings.
  • Ads: you can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings. Premium removes ads.
  • Block and report other players at any time.

If you are in the EU, UK or a country with similar laws, you also have the right to access your data, receive a copy (portability), correct it, have it erased, restrict or object to its processing, and withdraw consent. To use these rights, email us at [support email]. We will reply within one month. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know, delete and correct your personal information. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, except as performed by Google AdMob for ads shown to non-Premium users.

9. Children

Challengly is not intended for children under 13 (or under the minimum age for consenting to online services in your country, such as 16 in some EU countries). We do not knowingly collect data from children under that age. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Sign-in tokens are stored securely and refresh tokens are stored only in hashed form. Access to our servers is limited and protected with keys. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your data.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If the changes are important, we will tell you in the app before they take effect. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
