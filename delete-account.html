Delete your Challengly account

Last updated: October 3, 2026

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Challengly and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

By email

If you can't use the app, email redwatercc@gmail.com from the email address of your Google or Apple account with the subject "Delete my Challengly account". We may ask you to confirm the request. We delete the account within 30 days.

Have Premium? Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Cancel it first in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, photo and language (your profile is shown as "Deleted user").
  • Your Google or Apple sign-in link, sign-in tokens and push notification devices. Sign in with Apple access is revoked.
  • Your membership in all rooms. Rooms you own are handed over to another member, or closed if you were the only one.
  • Your global statistics and records, activity days, pending invitations and join requests, and your block list.

What is kept

  • Messages you sent and your past daily results stay in their rooms, shown as "Deleted user", so other players' history stays complete. They are no longer linked to any personal information.
  • Reports about content may be kept as long as needed to handle them.
  • Purchase records may be kept where accounting or tax law requires it.
  • Backups are overwritten within 30 days.

Signing in again later creates a new, empty account. See our Privacy Policy for details.
